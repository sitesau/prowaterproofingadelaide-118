What the inspection should settle first

The published process is simple: share project details, arrange a site inspection, receive a written quote, then have work completed to Australian Standards. For approval-minded owners, that means the inspection note becomes evidence: what was seen, what was assumed, what remains unseen, and which standard or compliance reference applies where relevant.
